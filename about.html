How I got into graphics/games

I got into games when I was a child because I was obsessed with playing Minecraft LAN after school at the Boys and Girls Club (and later on Animal Jam and such).

I ended up wanting to create my own games and to create experiences that brought people together like I had so I started learning how to write code.

I started with Minecraft modding but eventually moved to Roblox when I got my first laptop and fell in love with the platform. I loved how easy it was to get into but hard to master with novel problems being solved each day. I also loved how the platform was focused on social experiences and how it was a place where I could meet people from all over the world and make friends.

After spending I think about 5 years on the platform at this point I got burnt out. I had already accomplished a lot but felt like I was plateauing.

Thats when I got into studying Computer Science alone seperate from my software work.

This is my true love in life.

I was hungry for knowledge and understanding so I picked up things all over the place and one of them was Computer Graphics.

I ended up loving it and learning about it and just making cool stuff and theres always something new to learn and I just love it so much. I hope to continue to learn about and contribute to the field and hopefully make game engines for a living.
